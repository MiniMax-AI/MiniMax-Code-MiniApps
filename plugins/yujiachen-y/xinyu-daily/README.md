# 心屿 Xinyu Island (`xinyu-daily`)

English | [简体中文](README.zh-CN.md)

A cozy daily-mood Mini App that plays like a small indie game. A floating island grows as you tell
its keeper, a cloud named Xiaoyu, more about yourself. Each day you can draw an inspiration card,
stick a mood sticker, drop small joys into a jar, settle indecision with a slot machine, and hunt
for 19 hidden secrets guarded by Hengheng, a grumpy crab who calls himself the island bully.

Everything runs locally. The app makes no network requests and runs no Agent turns. The interface
is in Chinese.

*Preview uses synthetic data.*

## What you can do

- **Today's inspiration card**: answer three light questions, then flip a collectible card with a
  keyword, an energy score, four small stats, a colour, a number, things that suit today and things
  to set aside. Your answers, your birthday, and what Xiaoyu knows about you change the card. Rare
  cards are holographic. Content is generated by rules and is for fun and self-reflection only; it
  makes no predictions and gives no advice.
- **Mood stickers and journal**: one sticker per day, a sticker calendar, a 30-day mood curve,
  streak medals, a card collection album, and a wall of small joys.
- **Decision crusher**: a slot machine with a pullable lever, and a Book of Answers that gives the
  same answer to the same question on the same day.
- **Self-exploration**: twelve "about you" questions that grow the island (flowers, a tree, a
  house, a lighthouse, a windmill, a rainbow) and change its weather, season, and weekend props;
  a birthday-flower portrait; an eight-question energy-style quiz.
- **Exploration**: a wake-up opening, region banners, a daily journey checklist, and 19 secrets
  hidden around the island (poke the cloud, tap the moon, squeeze a cloud, catch a shooting star,
  and more). Each find makes Hengheng hand over a shell, tracked in a secrets log.
- Optional synthesized sound effects (Web Audio, no audio files), day/night sky that follows the
  clock, and `prefers-reduced-motion` support.

## Install

Copy this directory, including the hidden `.minimax-plugin/`, into the MiniMax Code plugins
directory as `xinyu-daily/` (`~/.minimax/plugins/xinyu-daily/` by default; see the root README's
Install section). Restart MiniMax Code, then open 心屿 from the Mini App entry in the right panel or
ask the Agent to "打开心屿".

## Data & access

- Files: reads its own files under `miniapp/client/`. Writes one file, `xinyu-state.json`, inside
  the Host-provided `context.dataDir` (atomic write through a temporary file). Nothing else.
- Network: none. The page only calls the app's own same-origin routes under `/api/`.
- Processes: none.
- Agent turns: none. No `agentAccess`.
- State stored in `xinyu-state.json`: nickname, birthday, daily mood check-ins and notes, drawn
  cards and favourites, small joys, Book of Answers history, quiz result, about-you answers,
  discovered secrets, and one-time onboarding flags. The browser's `localStorage` keeps only view
  preferences (current tab, sky mode, sound on/off).
- Reset: Settings → 清空所有数据 deletes all stored state.
